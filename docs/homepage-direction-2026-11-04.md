# Homepage direction — November 4, 2026

The owner rejected the 3D scroll experience. This supersedes the scene-related guidance in the November 3 refinement report.

## References viewed on Dribbble

- [Craftwork — Free Alexfolio](https://dribbble.com/shots/26873011-Free-Alexfolio-Portfolio-template): prominent personal identity, portrait-led composition, oversized readable type, restrained palette. Adapted the hierarchy, without copying the overlapping portrait treatment or assets.
- [Akib for ARQ Studio — Minimal Bento-Style Design](https://dribbble.com/shots/26301912-UI-UX-Portfolio-Minimal-Bento-Style-Design): modular content organization and clear separation of introduction, capabilities, and portfolio. Adapted the organization into a quiet four-column expertise index, without its cartoon imagery, metric tiles, or dense rounded cards.

## Implementation

- A direct introduction and personal portrait replace the 3D scene. Selected work and publications have visible entry points.
- Four linked areas of practice explain agents, orchestration, backend engineering, and research without a long pinned sequence.
- The selected project gallery and dedicated projects archive retain their visual language.
- Research, experience, writing, and contact follow a consistent reading order. The lower biography no longer duplicates the portrait.
- Removed the scene scripts, render poster, Three.js, GSAP, and Three.js types. The homepage requires no scene runtime or scroll interception.
- Existing publication reconciliation, case studies, research tables, citation export, writing revisions, and lazy search remain in place.
